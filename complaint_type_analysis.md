Based on the 2024 NYC 311 dataset, Illegal Parking was the most common complaint type, with 505,546 complaints.
In January and February, there were 41,124 and 38,792 Illegal Parking complaints, respectively.
In June and July, there were 46,370 and 39,867 complaints, respectively.
The total number of complaints increased from 79,916 in January–February to 86,237 in June–July, an increase of approximately 7.9%.
June had the highest number of complaints among the four months, while February had the lowest.
These results show that Illegal Parking complaints were more frequent in June–July than in January–February. However, further analysis would be needed to understand the reasons for this difference.
